What Is Drone Interception? Drone interception is the process of responding to a drone that may pose a safety, security, or operational concern. Depending on the situation and the authority of the organization involved, interception can include locating and approaching the drone operator, coordinating with law enforcement, restricting access to an area, or, for agencies with the appropriate legal authority, taking action against the aircraft itself.
